## primordial/primordial-chaos.md
- [UNSUPPORTED] primordial-chaos.md 「古希臘文的 chaos 跟「張開、打呵欠」是同一家族的字」[s3] — Theoi Khaos says only: "The word khaos means 'gap' or 'chasm' being the space between heaven and earth". It says nothing about "yawn". The etymology (χάσκω, "to gape or yawn") is correct, but it needs a lexicon or etymology source such as LSJ or Beekes. Alternatively, cut the sentence to 「字面意思是『裂口、深淵』」.
- [UNSUPPORTED] primordial-chaos.md how_to_recognize / few_images 「古代陶瓶與雕像幾乎沒有畫過卡厄斯」 — the card cites no source for this, and Theoi Khaos does not discuss art. The claim is plausible, but no source supports it.

## primordial/primordial-gaia.md

- [STYLE] primordial-gaia.md 「天空從此和大地分開。」[s2] — Hesiod's verse never says this. Only the translator's note says the myth "accounts for the separation of Heaven and Earth". Suggested wording: 「（後人常解釋為：天與地從此分開。）」

## titan/titan-cronus.md
- [UNSUPPORTED] titan-cronus.md 「教當地人種田、立法」[s3] — Vergil (both the Latin and the Dryden translation on the cited MIT page) has Saturn gathering the scattered people, giving laws and naming Latium. Before his arrival they did not know "the care of lab'ring oxen", but the poem never says he taught farming. Suggested fix: 「把散居山間的人聚集起來、為他們立法」. WHE Saturn [s5] does tie him to farming.
- [UNSUPPORTED] titan-cronus.md how_to_recognize (src [s4]) 「手拿鐮刀的大鬍子老人…後世常把他和『時間老人』混在一起…其實是兩個不同的名字」 — Theoi Kronos does not describe his iconography. It also says the opposite on the name point: "Kronos was essentially the same as Khronos… The name Kronos means simply time". The card's position matches the modern scholarly view, but the cited source contradicts it. Cite a different source.
- [STYLE] titan-cronus.md s3 URL — the line numbers (8.319–327) are Latin, but the URL points to the Dryden verse translation, which has no matching line numbers. The cite already notes this. A Latin or line-numbered text, such as Perseus, would make the citation checkable.
- The Saturnalia (17–23 December, gifts, masters serving slaves) and the temple as treasury are confirmed by WHE Saturn (Wasson, 16 Oct 2015).

## titan/titan-rhea.md
- [UNSUPPORTED] titan-rhea.md 「用山羊阿瑪爾忒亞的奶餵他」[s3] — Apollodorus 1.1.7 says only "fed the child on the milk of Amalthea". He never says she was a goat. The goat version appears elsewhere, for example Callimachus, Hymn 1.47–49, and Hyginus, Astronomica 2.13. Fix: 「用阿瑪爾忒亞的奶餵他」, or add a source for the goat.
- [STYLE] titan-rhea.md 「無法忘懷的悲痛」[s2] — the Evelyn-White translation has "unceasing grief" (πένθος ἄλαστον). A closer rendering is 「無盡的悲痛」.
- [STYLE] titan-rhea.md three_lines 「奧林帕斯第一代六位神的母親」 — Hades is normally not counted among the Olympians. Suggested fix: 「第一代六位宙斯兄姊的母親」.

## titan/titan-prometheus.md
- [WRONG] titan-prometheus.md 「宙斯選了脂肪那一堆，掀開一看全是骨頭，勃然大怒。」[s1] — in Hesiod, Zeus "saw and failed not to perceive the trick" (paragraph [545]) and chose the fat knowingly, already planning evil for men. The card implies that Zeus was fooled. Fix: 「宙斯其實看穿了詭計，卻還是伸手拿起白脂肪，看到底下的白骨，怒火中燒。」 The three_lines entry 「兩次騙過宙斯」 should also be softened, to something like 「兩次和宙斯鬥智」.
- [LINE] titan-prometheus.md 「普羅米修斯細數自己教會人類的一切，從觀星、算數、文字到馴馬」[s3] (1–122, 228–258) — the list of arts is at lines 436–506: astronomy at 447ff, numbers, letters and horses at 459ff, medicine and metals at 477–506. Lines 228–258 cover only saving mankind, blind hopes and fire. Fix: cite 1–122、228–258、436–506.
- [UNSUPPORTED] titan-prometheus.md family.parents variant 「埃斯庫羅斯說母親是忒彌斯」 src [s1] — s1 is Hesiod. The Themis claim is in Aeschylus, Prometheus Bound 18 and 209–211 ("my mother Themis, or Earth"). Fix: add [s3] and include line 209–211 in s3.
- [STYLE] titan-prometheus.md 「大茴香莖」 (domains.symbols and story) — in Chinese, 大茴香 usually means star anise (八角). The plant is giant fennel (narthex, Ferula communis). Suggested fix: 「巨茴香（阿魏屬植物）的空心莖」 or 「空心的茴香莖」.
- [STYLE] titan-prometheus.md 「把他用鎖鏈牢牢綁在柱子上」[s1] — the cited translation has "cruel chains, and drove a shaft through his middle". The Greek line is often read as "through the middle of a pillar", so 「柱子」 is defensible, but it does not match the cited translation. Optional note: 「（一說是用一根木樁穿過他身體）」.

- [UNSUPPORTED] titan-atlas.md disputed view 「…被梅杜莎的頭變成一座大山，也就是北非的阿特拉斯山」[s4] — Ovid places Atlas "far in the west" and never names Africa or the mountain range. The identification comes from Theoi Atlas [s6]: "the range of mountains in that part of the world bears the name of Atlas". Fix: move 「也就是北非的阿特拉斯山」 to an [s6] cite.
- Theog. 507–520 and 746–750, Odyssey 1.52–54, Apollodorus 2.5.11 (the apples are the eleventh labour) and 3.10.1 (the Pleiades, Maia) all check out. Theoi Atlas supports the punishment as a result of the Titan war.

## god/god-zeus.md
- [STYLE] god-zeus.md three_lines 「木星（Jupiter）、木衛四大衛星」 — 「木衛四」 is the standard Taiwan name for Callisto (Jupiter IV), so the phrase reads as "Callisto, a large moon". Fix: 「木星的四顆伽利略衛星」.
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-zeus.md greek_vs_roman 「希臘神話裡宙斯四處留情的故事，多半是羅馬人後來從希臘借來的。」[s8] — Britannica's Jupiter article does not say this. Either cite another source or delete the sentence.
- [STYLE] god-zeus.md greek_vs_roman 「執政官就職要向他獻白牛」[s8] — Britannica ties the white ox to the temple's dedication festival on 13 September, the day "the consuls originally took office". Suggested fix: 「每年 9 月 13 日神廟節慶，執政官（早期就在這天就職）向他獻白牛」.
- [LINK] god-zeus.md s6 — the cite names Iliad 1.571–578 and Theog. 927–929 and 188–206, but its URL is only the Iliad 5 page. Iliad 5 does confirm Dione as Aphrodite's mother ([363]) and "daughter of Zeus" ([311]). The other passages need their own URLs (HomerIliad1.html, HesiodTheogony.html).
- The consorts, the order of the children and 「最後才娶了希拉」 match Theog. 886–944. Hesiod also lists Eurynome, but omitting her is fine. Iliad 15 (lots), Apollodorus 2.4.1 (Danae) and Theoi Zeus (oaths, xenios, kingship) are confirmed.

## god/god-hera.md
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-hera.md 「她還因為氣宙斯獨自生出雅典娜，自己不靠丈夫生下了赫菲斯托斯」[s2] — Theog. 927–929 gives the reason only as "she was very angry and quarrelled with her mate". Athena is not named as the cause, although her birth from Zeus's head is described just before. The Athena motive is in Hesiod fr. 343 (Merkelbach–West) and the Homeric Hymn to Apollo 305–355. Fix: 「她和宙斯大吵一架，一氣之下不靠丈夫生下了赫菲斯托斯」, or add one of those sources.
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-hera.md greek_vs_roman 「羅馬的朱諾本來就是義大利自己的女神」[s7] — WHE Juno says "her exact origin is unknown" and stresses only that she is one of Rome's oldest deities. Fix: 「羅馬的朱諾是羅馬最古老的神祇之一，後來才和希拉混在一起」.
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-hera.md three_lines 「英文月份 June 很可能來自她的羅馬名朱諾」 — none of the card's sources mention it (WHE Juno does not). The claim is likely true, through Ovid, Fasti 6, so it needs a source or a today-item cite.
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-hera.md 「海克力斯的名字字面上是『希拉的榮耀』」 — the claim is uncited. Apollodorus 2.4.12 says only that the Pythia "first called him Hercules". The etymology is standard but has no source on this card.
- [STYLE] god-hera.md 「海克力斯的十二件苦差事，正是為了贖這個罪」[s5] — Apollodorus 2.4.12 says the Pythia imposed "ten labours". They became twelve because Eurystheus refused to count two. This is fine as shorthand.
- Iliad 14 (girdle, Sleep bribed with Pasithea, Ida, episode ends at [352]), Ovid Met. 1 (Io, Argus, peacock; the story runs from about 1.583 to 1.746), Apollodorus 2.4.5, 2.4.8 and 2.4.12, Theoi Hera (Trojan War, Argos and Samos) and WHE Juno (Moneta and money, Lucina, Matronalia on 1 March) are all confirmed.
