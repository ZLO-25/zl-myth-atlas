## story-pandora.md
- [UNSUPPORTED] story-pandora.md disputed view 2 「也有人認為希望本身也是一種災禍，因為它讓人持續忍受痛苦」 [s3]: Theoi「Pandora」 presents Hope only as good ("a single blessing to ease mankind's suffering"; Aeschylus "A great benefit"). It never says hope is an evil. The "hope is an evil" view, including Nietzsche's "prolongs man's torment", is in s4 (Wikipedia "Pandora's box"). Fix: change the cite to `src: [s3, s4]`, or to [s4] alone.
- [UNSUPPORTED] story-pandora.md body 「赫西俄德用的字是 pithos：一種半人高、用來儲存穀物或酒油的大陶罐。[s2]」: the Theoi translation of s2 says only "the great jar". The word pithos and its use are not there. s4 does say pithoi held "wine, oil, grain", but gives no size. Fix: cite [s4] and drop 「半人高」, or cite a source that gives the size.

Everything else checked out against s1 and s2: Hermes's "shameless mind and deceitful nature", Athena teaching weaving, Hope staying "under the rim of the great jar... by the will of... Zeus", "jar", never "box", and the Erasmus *pyxis* point in s4.

It has no wedding, no "not invited" and no inscription. The uninvited Eris is in Hyginus, *Fabulae* 92, and Apollodorus mentions the wedding at 3.13.5. ## story-oedipus.md
- [WRONG] story-oedipus.md panel 4 「他和一輛馬車上的老人發生爭執，失手殺了對方」[s1]: Apollodorus 3.5.7 says "Oedipus in a rage killed both Polyphontes and Laius". It was deliberate, not 失手 (accidental). Fix: 「一怒之下殺了對方和他的傳令官」.
- [UNSUPPORTED] story-oedipus.md panel 4 「在一條三岔路口」[s1]: Apollodorus says "in a certain narrow road". The crossroads ("the meeting of three roads") is Sophocles, s2. Fix: cite `src: [s1, s2]`.
- [UNSUPPORTED] story-oedipus.md panel 5 「什麼東西早上四條腿、中午兩條腿、晚上三條腿？」[s1]: Apollodorus's riddle is "What is that which has one voice and yet becomes four-footed and two-footed and three-footed?" It has no morning, noon or evening. That framing is a later popular version. Fix: 「什麼東西只有一種聲音，卻會變成四條腿、兩條腿、三條腿？」.
- [UNSUPPORTED] story-oedipus.md body 「被亞里斯多德等後世評論家視為悲劇的典範……像偵探一樣追查」[s2][s4]: s4 does not mention Aristotle or a detective. The WHE "Oedipus the King" definition (https://www.worldhistory.org/Oedipus_the_King/) does not mention them either. The Aristotle claim is true (*Poetics* 1452a–b), so cite the *Poetics* or another source.
- [LINK] story-oedipus.md s4 `https://www.worldhistory.org/Oedipus_Rex/`: this is a WHE search listing, not an article. The closest real page is https://www.worldhistory.org/Oedipus_the_King/.
- [STYLE] story-oedipus.md disputed (Sophocles) 「他用她衣服上的金別針刺瞎自己，離開底比斯流亡」[s2]: the MIT page text was cut off (62,779 characters) before the ending, so this could not be confirmed from the link. From the play itself: the golden brooches are correct, but at the end of *Oedipus Rex* Creon defers the exile until the god is consulted. The actual exile is in *Oedipus at Colonus*. Fix: 「並請求被放逐出底比斯」.
- Note: 「沒有孩子的科林斯國王」 is supported by Sophocles ("A childless man till then"), not by s1. Suggest adding s2 to panel 2.

Also confirmed: Laius's oracle, pierced ankles, Periboea naming him for his swollen feet, the Delphi oracle and leaving Corinth (s1), the plague (s2), and Epicaste hanging herself while Oedipus "abode as lord of the Cadmeans" with no blinding (Od. 11.271–280).

checked: 16 claims, 5 sources opened (s2 ending not readable)
