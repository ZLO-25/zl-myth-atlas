## cards/god/god-hermes.md
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-hermes 「荷米斯也是唯一可以自由往返人間和冥界的神。」[s4] — Odyssey 24.1–14 only shows Hermes leading the suitors' ghosts. Theoi Hermes calls him "the guide of the dead who led souls down into the underworld" and says nothing about him being the only one. — 改為「荷米斯也負責引導亡魂前往冥界。」
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-hermes how_to_recognize 「早期陶瓶上他有尖鬍子，後來多半是沒鬍子的青年」[s6] — Theoi: "depicted as either a handsome and athletic, beardless youth or as an older, bearded man". It does not tie these to periods. The claim is true in art history, but s6 does not support it. Theoi also says the two snakes came later ("white ribbons ... changed by later artists into two serpents"). — 改為「有時是留鬍子的成年男子，有時是沒鬍子的青年」或另引藝術史來源。
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-hermes greek_vs_roman 「5 月 15 日是商人向他祈求生意興隆的節日」「希臘荷米斯身上『小偷』『亡魂引路人』的那些面向，在羅馬就淡得多」[s7] — WHE: the May 15 festival was held "in commemoration of the founding of his temple". WHE says nothing about the Roman god losing Hermes's roles. It says instead that the two gods' stories intertwined and "it is often difficult to separate the two". — 改為「5 月 15 日是紀念他神廟落成的節日」，並刪去「淡得多」一句。
- [STYLE] god-hermes s7 cite 「Mark Wasson, 2018」 — the WHE author is Donald L. Wasson (2018). — 改為「Donald L. Wasson, 2018」。
checked: 22 claims, 6 sources opened (Theogony, Hymn 4, Hymn 19, Odyssey 24, Met. 1, Theoi Hermes, WHE Mercury). The rest match: born at dawn, lyre by midday, cattle stolen in the evening, 50 cows driven backwards, twig sandals, "born yesterday, feet soft", Zeus laughed, lyre given to Apollo and herds to Hermes, Argus lulled with the reed pipe then cut with a curved sword, eyes set in the tail of Juno's bird, merx, temple c. 495 BCE near the Circus Maximus.

- [LINE] god-hestia greek_vs_roman 「羅馬廣場上的圓形維斯塔神廟」[s4] — the round temple is in Plutarch, Numa 11.1, which is outside the cited 9.5–10.7. WHE calls it only a "shrine". — 改 cite 為「9.5–11.1」。
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-hestia greek_vs_roman 「她們從小入選」「聖火熄滅被視為大凶之兆」「象徵國家命脈」[s4, s6] — Plutarch 9.6–7 says only that a fire that goes out must be relit from the sun's rays, and does not call it an omen. ## cards/god/god-dionysus.md
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-dionysus greek_vs_roman 「在義大利快速擴散……前 186 年下令嚴格限制，大批信徒被捕或處死」[s7] — WHE Bacchus says only that "The Roman Senate bans the Cult of Bacchus" (timeline) and that its altars "were ordered to be suppressed". It mentions no spread across Italy, no arrests and no executions. The facts are true per Livy 39.8–19 and the Senatus consultum de Bacchanalibus. — 加引李維《羅馬史》39.8–19。
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-dionysus how_to_recognize 「古陶瓶上是有長鬍子的成年男子，後期則是柔美、半醉的年輕人」[s6] — Theoi lists a bearded "Indian Bacchus" and a youthful "Theban Bacchus" side by side as forms "in later works of art". It does not put them in this order. The claim is true in art history but not in s6. Attributes (thyrsus, kantharos, ivy, panther, satyrs, maenads) are supported.

- [STYLE] god-dionysus s7 cite 「Mark Wasson, 2023」 — the author is Donald L. Wasson (2023). — 改為「Donald L. Wasson, 2023」。
checked: 20 claims, 5 sources opened (Theogony 940–942, 947–949; Met. 3 Semele & Pentheus; Hymn 7; Theoi; WHE Bacchus). Supported: Juno disguised as the old nurse Beroe, Jupiter swears first, Semele burned, child carried in Jupiter's thigh (the Latin *insuitur* = "sewn in"), Agave first to attack Pentheus believing him a boar, Ariadne daughter of Minos.

## cards/god/god-hades.md
- [WRONG] god-hades 「他不會去人間害人，只是不讓死者離開……是後來的文化混合出來的形象。」[s6] — Theoi Haides calls him "fierce and inexorable", most hated of the gods by mortals, and says "Haides and Persephone inflicted Thebes with a deadly plague". It never discusses the devil. — 改為「他主要的職責是不讓死者離開冥界，並不是專門作惡的魔鬼」，並刪去「不會去人間害人」；「魔鬼形象是後世混合」需另找來源。
- [STYLE] god-hades 「冥界的大門有一隻可怕的看門犬（後來的作者稱牠為三頭犬克爾柏洛斯）」 — Hesiod himself names him Cerberus at Theogony 311, "the brazen-voiced hound of Hades, fifty-headed". Later authors gave him three heads, not the name. — 改為「赫西俄德在別處叫牠克爾柏洛斯，說牠有五十個頭；後來的作者才說牠有三個頭」。
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-hades greek_vs_roman 「狄斯．帕特（『富有的父親』）」「羅馬並沒有替他加上新的神話」[s6, s7] — WHE Pluto (Donald L. Wasson, 2023) does not mention Plouton, Dis Pater or the meaning. Theoi supports Plouton = "giver of wealth" and the dreaded name (via Plato), but not the Dis Pater meaning. That meaning is true (Dis < dives), but needs a source.
- [STYLE] god-hades three_lines 「讓人間從此有了冬天」 — Hymn to Demeter never says "winter". It says Persephone spends "the third part of the circling year" below and returns "with the flowers of spring". — 可改為「讓大地每年有一段時間不長作物」。
- [STYLE] god-hades s7 cite 「Mark Wasson, 2023」 — the author is Donald L. Wasson.
checked: 20 claims, 6 sources opened (Theogony, Iliad 15, Iliad 5, Hymn 2, Theoi Haides, WHE Pluto). Supported: the lot ("Hades won the murky darkness", earth and Olympus shared), Athena wearing the cap of Hades, the gift by Zeus, flower-gathering, the golden car, the barren earth, Hades not an Olympian, the cornucopia, the hound that lets ghosts in and devours any who try to leave.

## cards/god/god-janus.md
- [WRONG] god-janus 「詩人又問：為什麼新年要從你開始？他回答：一切開始都屬於我。」[s1] — In Fasti 1, when asked why he is honored first, Janus says "through me, who guard the thresholds, you may have access to whatever gods you please". On beginnings he says only "Omens are wont to wait upon beginnings". There is no "all beginnings belong to me". — 改為「詩人又問：祭拜其他神時，為什麼要先敬你？他回答：我守著門檻，你們要經過我，才能到達想祈求的神。」
- [LINK] god-janus s2 (Perseus URL) — the site refuses automated fetching (robots). I read the same passage at https://fgh.perseids.org/read/latinLit/phi0914/phi001/perseus-eng3/1.19.2-1.19.3, and the content is supported: built by Numa, open in war and closed in peace, closed only twice since Numa (after the First Punic War under T. Manlius, and after Actium by Augustus). The URL itself is probably fine for human readers.
- [STYLE] god-janus s3 cite 「Mark Wasson, 2015」 — the author is Donald L. Wasson (2015).
checked: 12 claims, 3 sources opened. Supported: the door has two fronts and the porter sees in and out; Janus guards heaven's gate; the 235 BCE and 29 BCE closures; no Greek counterpart; named before Jupiter in prayers.

## cards/monster/monster-medusa.md
