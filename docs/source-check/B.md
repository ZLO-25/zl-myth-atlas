## god-demeter.md
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-demeter.md 「入會者發誓保密，所以我們至今不知道儀式的細節。[s4]」 — Theoi「Demeter」says only that she "presided over the foremost of the Mystery Cults" and that it "promised its initiates the path to a blessed afterlife". It says nothing about an oath of secrecy or about the details being unknown. The Homeric Hymn (s3) does support secrecy: the rites are not to be "transgressed, probed, or spoken of". — Change the cite to [s3]. Keep 「所以我們至今不知道儀式的細節」 only as the author's own remark, or cite a modern source for it.
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-demeter.md greek_vs_roman 「羅馬人把本土的農業女神克瑞斯和狄蜜特視為同一位，連同她的故事（女兒被冥王擄走）一起接收」 — WHE「Ceres」says only "Her Greek counterpart is Demeter". It never calls Ceres native (本土) and never says the Romans took the story over. It just tells the Proserpina story. The blight/drought/famine, harvest and *cereal* parts are supported. — Either reword to 「羅馬的克瑞斯對應希臘的狄蜜特，也有女兒被冥王擄走的故事」, or cite a source on Ceres' Italic origin (e.g. Britannica「Ceres」).
- [WRONG] god-demeter.md 「黑帝斯臨行前讓她吃了幾顆石榴籽」 — the Hymn has a single seed. Persephone says she was made to eat "a pomegranate seed" (Greek ῥοιῆς κόκκον, singular). — 「讓她吃了一顆石榴籽」.
- (Minor note, no severity) 「從此有了冬天」 in three_lines: the Hymn says Persephone spends a third of the year below and comes back when the earth "blooms with ... flowers of spring". Reading her time below as winter is the usual interpretation and is acceptable.

checked: 16 claims, 4 sources opened (Theogony, Homeric Hymns 1 page, Theoi Demeter, WHE Ceres)

## god-athena.md
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-athena.md 「她把磨得像鏡子一樣的盾借給柏修斯，幫他斬下梅杜莎的頭」 [s5] — Apollodorus 2.4.2: "while Athena guided his hand and he looked with averted gaze on a brazen shield, in which he beheld the image of the Gorgon, he beheaded her." It does not say Athena lent the shield or that it was polished like a mirror. The rest is supported: Perseus "gave [the head] to Athena" and "Athena inserted the Gorgon's head in the middle of her shield". — 「柏修斯看著青銅盾牌上的倒影，由雅典娜引導他的手，斬下梅杜莎的頭」.
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-athena.md greek_vs_roman 「羅馬的工匠與學生在她的節日五日節（Quinquatrus）向她祈福」 and 「『戰爭女神』的面向是後來才跟著雅典娜加上去的」 — WHE「Minerva」says the five-day festival began on 19 March, took over Mars' festival and marked the start of the campaign season. It does not mention craftsmen or students at the festival. Its list of domains ends "and later, war", but it does not say the war aspect came through Athena. The Etruscan Menrva origin ("scholarly consensus"), the Capitoline Triad and the domains are supported. (Craftsmen and schoolchildren honouring Minerva at the Quinquatrus is in Ovid, *Fasti* 3.809–848, if a source is wanted.) — 「戰爭女神的面向是後來才有的」, and cite Ovid *Fasti* for the craftsmen and students.
- [LINK] god-athena.md s4 「荷馬《奧德賽》1.44–95、13.287–440」 — the URL is the Book 13 page only (Murray). Book 1 is on a different page (https://www.theoi.com/Text/HomerOdyssey1.html). The Book 13 passage is confirmed: "thou art far the best of all men in counsel and in speech, and I among all the gods am famed for wisdom and craft" [287].
- (Minor STYLE) 「橄欖也成了雅典最重要的作物」 is not in Apollodorus 3.14.1, which says the olive "is still shown in the Pandrosium". Theoi Athena does say the olive was "the greatest blessing of Attica", so this is acceptable.

checked: 17 claims, 7 sources opened (Theogony, Homeric Hymns 3 page, Apollodorus 3, Apollodorus 2, Odyssey 13, Theoi Athena, WHE Minerva)

## god-apollo.md
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-apollo.md 「沒有一塊土地敢讓她生產，怕得罪天后希拉」 [s2] — in the Homeric Hymn to Apollo the lands "greatly trembled and feared" to receive Phoebus himself. Delos fears he will scorn the rocky island. Hera appears only as the one who kept Eileithyia away out of envy. Hera's ban on the lands is in Callimachus' *Hymn to Delos* and Apollodorus 1.4.1. — 「沒有一塊土地敢收留她，都害怕這位即將出生的強大神祇」, or cite Callimachus or Apollodorus.
- [WRONG] god-apollo.md 「阿伽門農搶走並羞辱了阿波羅祭司的女兒」 [s4] — Iliad 1.11: "upon the priest Chryses the son of Atreus had wrought dishonour". The insult was to the priest, when Agamemnon refused the ransom for his daughter. — 「阿伽門農拒絕讓阿波羅的祭司贖回女兒，還羞辱了這位祭司」.
- [LINE] god-apollo.md s4 「《伊里亞德》1.8–52」 — "For nine days the missiles of the god ranged among the host" is line 53. — 1.8–53.
- [UNREACHABLE] god-apollo.md s6 Livy 4.25.3 (perseus.tufts.edu) — WebFetch was refused (robots_disallowed), and the retry failed the same way. A WebSearch turned up the passage in Spillan's translation: "A temple was vowed to Apollo for the health of the people". This matches 4.25.3 (433 BC).
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-apollo.md greek_vs_roman 「人們為『醫者阿波羅』許願建廟」 — Livy's text calls the god simply "Apollo", with the temple vowed "pro valetudine populi". "Medicus" is a later epithet. — 「人們為了民眾的健康向阿波羅許願建廟」.
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-apollo.md greek_vs_roman 「後來奧古斯都把阿波羅當成自己的守護神，在帕拉丁丘上替他蓋了神廟」 [s6] — Livy book 4 cannot contain this. The claim is true (Suetonius, *Augustus* 29; *Res Gestae* 19). — Add a source for it.
- [WRONG] god-apollo.md three_lines 「奧運與比賽頒發『桂冠』的習俗也和他有關」 — the ancient Olympic victors were crowned with olive (kotinos), not laurel. The laurel crown belonged to Apollo's Pythian Games. Ovid (s3) has Apollo say that laurel will crown Roman triumphs. — 「德爾菲的皮提亞競技與羅馬凱旋式的『桂冠』都和他有關」.
- [STYLE] god-apollo.md tripod in symbols (「德爾菲的三腳鼎」) — Theoi Apollon does not list it (it appears only in a vase title). It is defensible as Delphic iconography.
- The how_to_recognize details "beardless youth" and "perfect ideal of youthful manliness" are supported by Theoi. Theoi does not mention his usual nudity, but that is not a problem.

checked: 18 claims, 6 sources opened (Theogony, Homeric Hymns 1 page, Ovid Met. 1, Iliad 1, Apollodorus 3, Theoi Apollon; Livy unreachable, checked via a secondary site)

## god-artemis.md
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-artemis.md 「阿提米絲射死了七個女兒」 [s4] — in Ovid (6.146–312) Apollo is named as the one who shoots the sons. The daughters are killed without the archer being named. Apollodorus 3.5.6 says plainly that "Artemis shot the daughters". — Add Apollodorus 3.5.6 as a source.
- [LINE] god-artemis.md s3 「奧維德《變形記》3.138–252」 — the "some thought her too cruel / others praised her chastity" remark that the card quotes is at 3.253–255 in the Latin. — 3.138–255.
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-artemis.md how_to_recognize 「文藝復興以後的畫常在她頭上畫一彎新月」 (src s6) — Theoi says the crescent "above her forehead" belongs to her moon-goddess form, which is ancient. It says nothing about the Renaissance. — 「作為月亮女神時，頭上常有一彎新月」.
- [STYLE] god-artemis.md greek_vs_roman 「後來她才和阿提米絲合而為一」 — Britannica: Diana "early became identified with Artemis". Everything else is supported: the woodland origin, Nemi, the runaway-slave priest, protector of slaves, the 13 August holiday for slaves, and the moon taken over later. — 「她很早就和阿提米絲合而為一，後來也接收了月亮女神的身分」.
- [STYLE] god-artemis.md 「女神讓海上無風，船隊無法出航」 [s5] — Apollodorus Epitome 3.21 has the fleet "windbound" (ἄπλοια, unable to sail). It does not say the sea was calm. — 「女神讓船隊無法出航」.
- The elder-twin claim (「雙胞胎姊姊」) is supported by Theoi Artemis ("the first of the twins born").

checked: 15 claims, 7 sources opened (Theogony, Homeric Hymns 3 page, Ovid Met. 3, Ovid Met. 6, Apollodorus Epitome, Theoi Artemis, Britannica Diana)

## god-ares.md
- [LINE] god-ares.md s3 「《伊里亞德》5.846–909」 — Athena stepping onto Diomedes' chariot (「雅典娜坐上...戰車」) comes earlier, at line 835ff. — 5.835–909.
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-ares.md 「希臘人對戰爭的看法很矛盾：需要勇氣，卻害怕失控的殺戮，阿瑞斯代表的正是後者。[s5]」 — Theoi Ares says he is "not so much the god of war as of its tumult, confusion, and horrors" and contrasts him with Athena's "thoughtfulness and wisdom in the affairs of war". It does not discuss the Greek attitude as needing courage while fearing slaughter. The second half is supported. — 「阿瑞斯代表的是戰爭中混亂、恐怖與殺戮的一面，和雅典娜的謀略恰好相反」.
- [STYLE] god-ares.md domains symbols 「狗」 — Theoi lists the serpent, the vulture and owls, but not the dog. (Dogs sacrificed to Enyalios are in Pausanias 3.14.9.) Consider replacing 「狗」 with 「蛇」.
- [STYLE] god-ares.md how_to_recognize 「後期雕像則是沒鬍子的年輕人」 — Theoi gives both types ("mature, bearded warrior ... or a nude, beardless youth") without dating them. Low priority.
- Everything else checks out: Hesiod 921–923, 933–937 ("Panic and Fear ... with Ares", and Harmonia), Iliad 5 ("loud as nine thousand warriors or ten thousand", "thou renegade ... most hateful"), Odyssey 8, and WHE Mars (Cartwright 2014: "second only to Jupiter", "more level-headed", father of Romulus and Remus, March, campaign rites, the disputed agricultural role).

checked: 20 claims, 6 sources opened (Theogony, Iliad 5, Odyssey 8, Theoi Ares, WHE Mars; Hesiod reused)

## god-aphrodite.md
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-aphrodite.md 「在一場婚禮上，不請自來的爭吵女神丟下一顆金蘋果，上面寫著『給最美的女神』」 [s5] — Apollodorus Epitome 3.2 says only "Strife threw an apple as a prize of beauty to be contended for by Hera, Athena, and Aphrodite". It has no wedding scene, no uninvited Eris and no inscription. Those details come from Hyginus, *Fabulae* 92 and Lucian. The promises (kingdom, victory, Helen) and Zeus sending them to Paris are supported. — Add Hyginus *Fab.* 92 as a source, or reword to 「爭吵女神丟下一顆金蘋果，作為最美女神的獎品」.
- [LINE] god-aphrodite.md s2 「荷馬《伊里亞德》5.370–371」 (cited for 「宙斯與女神狄奧涅的女兒」) — 5.370–371 has only "her mother Dione". Zeus is named as her father elsewhere: 5.312 ("daughter of Zeus") and 5.426–428 (Zeus calls her "my child"). — 5.312, 5.370–371, 5.426–428.
- [STYLE] god-aphrodite.md 「特洛伊王族的牧羊人安喀塞斯」 — Hymn 5: Anchises "was tending cattle ... among the steep hills of many-fountained Ida". — 「放牛的安喀塞斯」 or 「在伊達山上放牧的安喀塞斯」.
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-aphrodite.md greek_vs_roman 「凱撒所屬的尤利烏斯家族更自稱是她的直系後代」 — WHE「Venus」(Garcia 2013) says Iulus was an ancestor and "The Gens Julia was the family that included Julius Caesar". It does not say plainly that the family claimed descent from Venus, but it is strongly implied, and Venus Genetrix, Venus Victrix and Pompey's temple of 55 BCE are all supported. Low priority.
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-aphrodite.md effects hero-aeneas 「羅馬人因此自稱愛神後代」 [s4] — the Homeric Hymn to Aphrodite does not mention Rome. — Cite s7 for the Roman half.
- Also checked and fine: Hesiod 188–206 (Cythera, then Cyprus, grass under her feet), Odyssey 8 (invisible bonds), Theoi Eros as her son.

checked: 18 claims, 7 sources opened (Theogony, Iliad 5, Odyssey 8, Homeric Hymns 3 page, Apollodorus Epitome, Theoi Aphrodite, WHE Venus)

## god-hephaestus.md
- [WRONG] god-hephaestus.md s8 cite 「World History Encyclopedia「Vulcan」（Mark Wasson, 2023）」 — the article's author is Donald L. Wasson (19 April 2023). — （Donald L. Wasson, 2023）.
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-hephaestus.md greek_vs_roman 「羅馬的伍爾坎本來就是很古老的義大利火神…人們拜他，是希望他不要放火…伍爾坎節在 8 月 23 日，正是夏末乾熱、穀倉最怕失火的時候。和希臘的工匠神合併後，他才也有了鐵匠的形象。」 — WHE「Vulcan」supports only "god of fire and forge", "probably among the most dreaded of all the gods" (volcanoes), temples outside the city "for fear of fire", and the Vulcanalia on 23 August. A targeted search of the article found no mention of Italic origin, granaries or harvest, worship to avert fire, or how he later took on the smith role. (Britannica「Vulcan」covers the destructive fire and the granary timing of the Vulcanalia.) — Replace the source with Britannica「Vulcan」(https://www.britannica.com/topic/Vulcan-Roman-god), or cut the text down to what WHE says.
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-hephaestus.md 「他替眾神蓋宮殿」 [s3] — Iliad 18 mentions only his own bronze house. The palaces he built for the gods are in Iliad 1.605–608 ("each one in his own house, which the famed lame god Hephaestus had built"). — Add 《伊里亞德》1.605–608.
- [STYLE] god-hephaestus.md 「是海中女神忒提斯收留了他」 — Iliad 18: "had not Eurynome and Thetis received me". — 「忒提斯與歐律諾墨收留了他」.
- [STYLE] god-hephaestus.md 「赫西俄德說，希拉氣宙斯獨自生出雅典娜，於是…自己生下了赫菲斯托斯」 [s1] — Theogony 927–929: "Hera without union with Zeus — for she was very angry and quarrelled with her mate — bare famous Hephaestus". The anger is there, but the text does not say explicitly that Athena's birth caused it, although the order of the passage implies it. — 「希拉和宙斯吵架、氣憤之下，不靠丈夫自己生下了赫菲斯托斯」.
- [UNSUPPORTED] god-hephaestus.md how_to_recognize 「醉醺醺、騎驢被帶回奧林帕斯」 (src s7) — Theoi Hephaistos shows the donkey only in vase captions ("Dionysus and Hephaestus riding donkey"). The page does not narrate the drunken return. Low priority. The oval cap, the chiton leaving the right shoulder bare, and the hammer and tongs are supported.
- [STYLE] three_lines/story 「第一個女人潘朵拉」 [s6] — Works and Days 60ff supports earth mixed with water, but it never calls her the first woman. Theogony 590 ("from her is the race of women") would support that.
- Also fine: Iliad 1.590–594 (the fall to Lemnos, Hephaestus "striving to save" his mother), Iliad 18 (Charis as wife, nine years in the cave, self-moving tripods, golden handmaidens, the scenes on the shield), Theogony 945–946 (Aglaea), Odyssey 8.

checked: 19 claims, 8 sources opened (Theogony, Iliad 1, Iliad 18, Odyssey 8, Works and Days, Theoi Hephaistos, WHE Vulcan ×2)
